# 駅の縄張りマップ 【首都圏編】 data (tokyo)

Generated by https://github.com/isshiki/eki-walk with ekiwalk from https://github.com/isshiki/rail-gap-map (see docs/method.md and docs/data-sources.md). Published with the map on the gh-pages branch; copied there as README.md of each data folder.

- Territories, band, pale areas, 15-minute outlines, borders, per-cell walking distances and the overview (`territories.geojson`, `band.geojson`, `far.geojson`, `lines.geojson`, `iso/`, `cells/`, `adjacency.json`, `overview.json`): derived from OpenStreetMap (Geofabrik kanto-261001) — © OpenStreetMap contributors — and made available under the Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0: https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/
- Stations and administrative areas: 「国土数値情報（鉄道データ）」（国土交通省）（https://nlftp.mlit.go.jp/ksj/gml/datalist/KsjTmplt-N02-2025.html）、「国土数値情報（行政区域データ）」（国土交通省）（https://nlftp.mlit.go.jp/ksj/gml/datalist/KsjTmplt-N03-2026.html）を加工して作成 (CC BY 4.0).
- `places.json` (search): 「アドレス・ベース・レジストリ」（デジタル庁）（https://www.digital.go.jp/policies/base_registry_address）を加工して作成 (公共データ利用規約 第1.0版 / PDL1.0, compatible with CC BY 4.0), with station names from 国土数値情報 (above). Most station readings come from OpenStreetMap `name:ja-Hira` (ODbL 1.0); some were added by the author (configs/station-readings.toml).

These data were edited and processed by the author of eki-walk; they are not published by the Government of Japan.
